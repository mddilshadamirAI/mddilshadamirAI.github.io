The Founder's Journey

The 25-Day Sprint: In early April 2026, I committed to a 180-day sprint. Within the first 25 days, I moved beyond basic Python to have some app with real interface. I learned to self-host my infrastructure using Docker basics, turning a simple laptop into a development powerhouse.

Building the Lab: I didn't just study; I deployed. From a calculator app on GitHub Pages to a hypothetical full Clinic Management System on Streamlit, I proved that a "student" could deliver professional-grade tools in weeks.
